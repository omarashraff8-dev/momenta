Our story

Designed with heart.
Finished with obsession.

Momenta began with a simple belief: a celebration should feel like the people at its centre — never like a catalogue.

For almost a decade we've designed engagements, katb ketab ceremonies and weddings across Cairo and the North Coast. We sketch, source, build and style every element ourselves, so the room you walk into is exactly the one we promised you.
